01 / THE LANDSCAPE

Naming things matters. Traditional SEO has radically changed.

Direct answers on Google results pages (AIO) cause over half of all searches to end without a click. Google has shifted its model, and if AI can't confidently understand what you are, what you do, and what you sell, it won't surface you.

"Marketeers want to appeal to emotions, to needs, to provide solutions—they need to continue to do that. They also need to ensure that what they are selling, to whom, and for how much is also delivered unambiguously. Otherwise LLMs in search or procurement could miss or muddle your message."
02 / THE PARADIGM SHIFT

From Rich Results to Machine Maps

Generative search engines don't just index text strings; they synthesise structured entities and measure relationships. Structured markup on the page survives the journey into the model's context layer—it isn't discarded in favour of raw prose.

03 / THE CORE PROBLEM

Confusion Causes Chaos

Internal inconsistency is our biggest blind spot in the generative age. Relying on contradictory or misaligned labels weakens entity clarity and causes AI engines to degrade confidence in your brand positioning.

04 / THE STRATEGY

Beyond App Schema: Build a Coherent Knowledge Graph

Schema is the medium, not the message. Agree on the shared ontology first, then express it consistently through Schema and let everything else inherit from it.

05 / GOVERNANCE & STANDARDISATION

Schema as an Operational Framework

Standardised Schema ensures page content, metadata, and site architecture work in harmony, preventing future content drift and intent overlap before they ever hit production.

06 / SUMMARY & ACTION PLAN

The Way Forward: Clarity, Organisation, Discovery

01

Standardise Vocabulary

Establish agreed naming conventions for core products and software across all team silos.

02

Implement Cross-Entity Schema

Deploy agreed Schema for organisations, apps, authors, and any entity touchpoints.

03

Enforce Single Canonical Paths

Protect entity authority and stop internal confusion before publishing.

Closing Thought

The organisations that will win in the AI era won't be the ones with the snappiest marketing copy. They'll be the ones whose entities are described so unambiguously that no machine, search engine, procurement agent, or LLM could mistake them.
