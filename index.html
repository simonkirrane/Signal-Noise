Core Narrative

Traditional SEO is gone.

Google has shifted from a doorway into a final destination. Search engines now provide direct answers via AI summaries (AIO), causing over half of all searches to end without a click[cite: 4].

AI Procurement is Coming

AI Agents are looking at procurement. If LLMs can't parse your data, you stop turning up. 64% of CSCOs and COOs report GenAI is transforming their supply chain workflows[cite: 4].

Source: IBM — AI Agents in Procurement

From Rich Snippets to Machine Maps

Schema is now a translation layer. Generative engines don't index text strings—they synthesize structured entities. Inference is much less reliable than instruction[cite: 4].

Source: Aggarwal et al. (Princeton/Georgia Tech) — GEO: Generative Engine Optimization (arXiv:2311.09735)

Confusion Can Cause Chaos

Internal inconsistency is our biggest blind spot. Marketing calls it a 'Product', docs call it an 'App', and pages lack schema. This erodes entity confidence[cite: 4].

Source: Space & Story — The Complete Guide to Structured Data; TallmanCode — Schema JSON-LD & LLMs

Keyword Cannibalisation

Kolekti used 'Confluence' as a bellwether. When we cannibalised the term with two descriptions, ranking plummeted. Consolidation was the only way to recover[cite: 4].

Source: Studio 36 Digital — Keyword Cannibalisation Study | SEOsilla / Ahrefs Framework

Build a Coherent Knowledge Graph

We need an interconnected graph to link entities: Organisation → Author; Software → Features. Schema is the bedrock to translate into all other messaging[cite: 4].

Source: WordLift / AI Marketing Club — Build a Knowledge Graph That AI Can't Ignore

Governance: The Way Forward

1. Standardise Vocabulary. 2. Implement Cross-Entity Schema. 3. Enforce Single Canonical Paths. We are building the map that AI relies on to navigate the space[cite: 4].

Source: Search Engine Land — What Is the Knowledge Graph?
