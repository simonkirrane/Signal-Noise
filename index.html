The Paradigm Shift

From Rich Snippets to Machine Maps

Google Schema used to be about ranking top in the list of blue links (SERPs)[cite: 5]. Today, it acts as a critical layer of translation between human-focused web content and AI data engines[cite: 5].

Generative search engines and LLMs don't just index text strings—they synthesize structured entities and measure relationships[cite: 5]. Inference is much less reliable than instruction.

Keyword Cannibalisation Can Inform The Debate

In the traditional SEO era, if we designated two pages for a single term, Google split rankings[cite: 5]. Today, machines lack nuance when handling mixed signals[cite: 5].
